## 📌 About the Project

GUESSR is a fun, interactive number-guessing game with a pirate-inspired theme. Challenge yourself to discover the secret number before you run out of attempts!

Choose your difficulty level, use hints wisely, and test your guessing skills in this exciting mini-game.

## ✨ Features

- 🎯 **Three Difficulty Levels** – Easy, Medium, and Hard.
- 🔢 **Secret Number Challenge** – Guess the randomly generated number.
- 💡 **Helpful Hints** – Get clues to guide your guesses.
- ❤️ **Limited Attempts** – Make every guess count.
- 🏆 **Score Tracking** – Track your wins and best performance.
- 📜 **Recent Games** – Review your previous attempts and results.
- 🎉 **Interactive Effects** – Enjoy animations and celebratory effects.
- 💾 **Local Storage** – Save your game statistics in your browser.
- 📱 **Responsive Design** – Play on desktop and mobile devices.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript
- Browser Local Storage

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Open GUESSR using the live website link above.
2. Select your preferred difficulty level.
3. Enter your guess for the secret number.
4. Follow the hints to determine whether your next guess should be higher or lower.
5. Find the correct number before your attempts run out.
6. Play again and try to improve your score!
